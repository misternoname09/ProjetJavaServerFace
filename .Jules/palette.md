## 2026-06-22 - [Polish Student Management UX]
**Learning:** In legacy JSF applications, UX improvements often require fixing underlying functional bugs (e.g., incorrect EL variable names, case-sensitive method references, and invalid/nested form tags) to ensure the enhanced components actually work.
**Action:** Always verify backing bean method names (case-sensitive) and entity property names before applying UX enhancements to JSF/PrimeFaces components.
