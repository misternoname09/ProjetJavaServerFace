## 2026-08-30 - Form Accessibility and Navigation in PrimeFaces Forms
**Learning:** Connecting `p:outputLabel` components to input fields with explicit `for` and `id` attributes enables screen reader accessibility and label-click focusing, while providing an `immediate="true"` Cancel button ensures users can safely navigate away from forms without validation blockages.
**Action:** When working with PrimeFaces forms, always explicitly bind `for` and `id` on labels/inputs and include an immediate cancel action.
