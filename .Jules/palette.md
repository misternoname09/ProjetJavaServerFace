## 2026-06-21 - [Accessibility & Form UX]
**Learning:** Legacy JSF/PrimeFaces applications often suffer from broken form semantics (nested forms, invalid tag syntax) and lack of accessibility attributes (aria-labels on links, disconnected labels).
**Action:** When improving legacy forms, prioritize fixing functional bugs (e.g., variable mismatches, case-sensitive action references) alongside structural improvements (panelGrid, non-nested forms) and accessibility (ARIA, connected labels) for a complete fix.
