## 2025-05-14 - UX Refinement Constraints in Legacy JSF
**Learning:** In legacy projects with significant structural debt (like nested forms or invalid tags), it is tempting to perform a full cleanup. However, micro-UX improvements must strictly adhere to line limits (e.g., < 50 lines) to remain within scope and facilitate easier review.
**Action:** Prioritize critical functional fixes and accessibility additions (ARIA labels, dynamic confirmation dialogs) over broad structural refactoring when working under strict line-count constraints.
